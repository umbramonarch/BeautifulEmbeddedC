Physical quantities (millivolts, duty cycle in permille, frequency in Hz) are modeled with single-field unit wrapper structs (`Millivolts_t`, `DutyPermille_t`, `FreqHz_t`) and/or explicit unit suffixes (`_mv`, `_permille`, `_hz`), with zero `float` or `double` types
